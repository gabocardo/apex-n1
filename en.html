01 / THE PROJECT

A real challenge.
Built together.

HOW IT STARTED

A flowpack machine or a palletizer? That was Festo’s challenge to our class at FIAP. We chose packaging and brought the N1 to life.

THE PROBLEM

The product is ready. What about packaging?

An order ready to ship. A machine at a standstill. During packaging, faults and difficult adjustments can hold up the entire operation.

That was our starting point: how could we combine efficiency, ease of operation and a reasonable cost?

THE SOLUTION

Every choice has a purpose.

In the N1, a NEMA 17 stepper motor drives movement and an ESP32 controls the system. The web dashboard and portable ESP controller with an LCD give the operator a clearer view of the machine.

How did we choose the components?

We considered each component’s purpose, our budget and how easy the machine would be to use, maintain and improve.

THE CHALLENGE THAT INSPIRED US

Learn. Build. Apply.

Festo set the challenge. We connected what we learned in class to the decisions behind an automation project.

Why is Festo part of our story?

Festo works in automation and, through Festo Didactic, technical education. For us, the challenge meant thinking through the machine’s operation, components and integration.

CONTROL / CONNECTION / TELEMETRY

Two interfaces.
One connected machine.

From the browser dashboard to the portable controller, we developed ways to monitor the N1 and control the process.

01 / WEB DASHBOARD

The process on screen.

We also built a web control interface for the machine. The dashboard shows operating status, sealing plate temperature and the number of packages produced.

  • Automatic cycleControls to start and stop the cycle.
  • Stage-by-stage controlIndividual activation of mechanisms such as the dosing unit, film feeder and sealer.
  • Operation controlsEmergency stop and machine unlock commands in the interface.

02 / ESP + LCD

Operation
in the palm of your hand.

A portable module with an ESP and LCD screen, connected to all the other microcontrollers in the machine to monitor and control the N1.

  • Battery and chargingWe added a battery and a charging port to the module.
  • Temperature and voltageSensor readings can be monitored in real time on the screen.
  • Controls at handProcess stage activation and an emergency stop command.
